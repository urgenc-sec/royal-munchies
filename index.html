The Manifesto

The New Table

Cannabis has moved into the penthouse. The people who run companies, close deals and carry the heaviest mental loads now end their day the elevated way — it is how they set the weight down and find their balance again.

And when the appetite arrives, they don't reach for fast food. They never did. They reach for food with craft in it: real ingredients, dishes built by chefs who care — food chosen the way they choose everything, with quality and longevity in mind.

Because an elevated palate notices everything: the salt, the smoke, the texture, the finish. The high doesn't lower the standard. It raises it.

Royal Munchies is the guide for that table — written for the affluent connoisseur, open to every elevated food lover who refuses to waste an appetite on the mediocre.

Every dish here is judged the way it will actually be eaten: elevated.
If it doesn't sing when you're high, it doesn't make the list.
